## Key facts

- The UK is a parliamentary democracy under a constitutional monarchy.
- King Charles III is the current monarch and Head of State; the monarch is politically impartial.
- The Prime Minister is Head of Government and leads the Cabinet.
- Parliament meets at Westminster and has two Houses: the elected House of Commons and the appointed House of Lords.
- The textbook states that the Commons has 650 MPs and Magna Carta has 63 rules/clauses.
- A bill is a proposal for a law. Parliament examines and approves legislation; the Lords can scrutinise bills and suggest amendments. Royal Assent is the final constitutional stage for a bill to become an Act.
- Magna Carta was agreed in 1215 under King John. It is associated with the principle that everyone, including the king, must obey the law, and with justice, privileges and rights.
